Data methodology

Campaign finance data and personal financial disclosures are different reporting systems. InsiderGov keeps them separate. The platform does not estimate personal wealth or manufacture missing financial records.

InsiderGov is an independent informational project and is not affiliated with any government agency or political campaign.
