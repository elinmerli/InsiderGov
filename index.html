ABOUT THE PLATFORM

About InsiderGov

Independent public-information tracking platform.
Our mission

InsiderGov aims to make public political financial information easier to access, understand and compare.

Data integrity

Information should be traceable to official sources. Missing data must never be replaced with invented statistics.

Campaign finance

FEC records describe campaign fundraising and spending. They do not represent politicians' personal investments.

Personal disclosures

House and Senate financial disclosures are separate records and may contain estimated asset ranges rather than exact values.
