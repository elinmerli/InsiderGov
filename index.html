Campaign finance

Campaign receipts and expenditures are not personal stock trades or personal wealth. FEC data is not connected in this version, so no campaign totals are displayed as if they were verified.

Open official FEC campaign finance data ↗

Official bulk datasets are updated on schedules that vary from daily to weekly. A separate data ingestion process is required to calculate reliable campaign statistics.

Analytics methodology

Statistics on this page are calculated only from records actually returned by the connected source. They are not a census of all congressional activity.

About InsiderGov

InsiderGov tracks publicly available financial disclosure records. It does not provide investment advice and does not infer intent from reported transactions.

Data source

Congressional disclosure records: Veridion Disclosure API. API documentation ↗

Official House documents: House Clerk ↗

Official Senate documents: Senate eFD ↗

Campaign finance: Federal Election Commission ↗

Important limitations

The anonymous API tier is limited to recent filings and a small number of rows per request. Periods longer than 30 days are not represented as complete historical data. Missing records must not be interpreted as zero activity.
