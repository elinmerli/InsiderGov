ABOUT THE PLATFORM

About InsiderGov

Making public political finance information easier to explore.
OUR MISSION

Transparency through accessible public data.

InsiderGov is an independent interface for exploring publicly available information about U.S. political finance. It brings campaign finance summaries and official financial disclosure resources into one place.

01 / CAMPAIGN FINANCE

FEC public data

Candidate fundraising and spending summaries are retrieved from the Federal Election Commission API.

Explore FEC data ↗
02 / DISCLOSURES

Official reports

House and Senate disclosure databases are linked directly so users can verify original filings.

House disclosure portal ↗
Data note: campaign contributions and committee spending are not the same as a politician's personal investments or stock transactions. Disclosure reports may contain estimated value ranges and reporting delays. Always consult original filings.
